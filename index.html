What we build

A job runs in three stages. We work all three.

Most homeowners end up coordinating a framer, a deck guy and a trim carpenter on the same project, then refereeing when the work doesn't line up. RMN carries the job from the first stud to the last piece of base shoe.

Stage 01 — Rough Structure. Everything that gets covered up later.

Framing

Walls, floor systems, roof framing and additions — laid out 16″ on center, plumb, square and ready for the trades that follow.

Structural repairs

Sistering joists, replacing rotted rim and sill, correcting sagging floors, rebuilding headers and load-bearing walls.

Subfloor & sheathing

Squeak-free subfloor, wall and roof sheathing, blocking and backing set where the finish carpentry will need it.

Basement build-out

Furring and framing basement walls, soffits for ductwork and plumbing, and the rough openings for new doors.

Stage 02 — Exterior Weather, wear and everything outside the sheathing.

Decks & stairs

New decks, rebuilds and resurfaces — ledger flashed properly, joists hung, stringers cut on site, boards run to a straight line.

Railings & privacy

Code-height rail, posts through the framing rather than bolted to a board, pergolas and privacy screens.

Soffit, fascia & trim

Rotted fascia and soffit replaced, exterior door and window trim, porch columns and ceilings.

Doors & windows

New units set and shimmed square, old openings reframed, entry and patio doors hung so they latch the same way in January and July.

Stage 03 — Finish The part everyone actually looks at.

Interior trim

Base, casing, crown and chair rail — coped inside corners, tight miters, scarf joints landed on framing.

Built-ins & shelving

Bookcases, benches, mudroom lockers, closet systems and mantels built to fit the wall they're going on.

Stairs & handrail

Treads and risers, skirt board, newels and handrail — including retrofitting a rough basement stair into a finished one.

Doors & hardware

Interior doors hung, casing wrapped, hinges and hardware set, and old doors re-fit to openings that have moved.

… and everything in between. If it's cut, fastened or fit on a jobsite, ask — odds are it's on the list.

How it goes

Four steps, no surprises.

The order matters here — each step exists so the next one can't go sideways. You'll know the scope and the number before anybody picks up a saw.

  1. Step 01

    You call

    Tell us what you're looking at. A few minutes on the phone usually sorts out whether it's a two-day fix or a two-week build.

  2. Step 02

    Walkthrough

    We come out, measure, and look at what's behind the finish surface — because that's where the surprises live.

  3. Step 03

    Written estimate

    Scope, materials and price in writing, itemized by stage, so you can see exactly what you're paying for.

  4. Step 04

    Build & walk it

    We build it, clean up behind ourselves, and walk the finished work with you before the last invoice goes out.
